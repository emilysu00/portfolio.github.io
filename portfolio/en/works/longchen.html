/Long Cheng Paper Co. CIS - Concept

We aim to convey the company's leading advantage in resource recycling and regeneration technology through design, while emphasizing its core philosophy of "regeneration, circulation, and carbon reduction."
The design direction revolves around four key concepts—environmental protection, circulation, paper materials, and industry. While showcasing the brand’s sustainability spirit, we also highlight its professional image as an industrial paper manufacturer. Through the development of logos, brand colors, typography, auxiliary graphics, and application designs, we establish a comprehensive corporate identity system. This ensures greater consistency and recognizability, allowing the brand to be flexibly applied across various corporate image and product scenarios.
We hope to not only optimize visual identity but also deepen the brand value of Rongcheng Paper Industry, making it more differentiated in the market and strengthening consumer recognition of environmental protection and the circular economy.
