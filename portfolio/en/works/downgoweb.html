/Down Go Website - Concept

This project extends the concept of mountain climbing safety and emergency self-rescue, integrating information into an interactive website. Users can learn climbing safety knowledge through a gamified experience and directly purchase related equipment.


    Introduction Page: A typing animation introduces the website’s purpose, creating a reading experience similar to a book preface, offering users an immersive opening.


    Homepage: Combines mouse-tracking interaction to create subtle dynamic changes in the main visual, enhancing visual appeal and providing users with a smooth, layered exploration experience.


    Mountain Climbing Preparation

  • Climbing Gear: Features a disassembled backpack visual. Users can click on different categories to view the necessary equipment for each layer, intuitively understanding how items are packed.

  • Injury Bandaging: Users can click to select an injured area and engage in simple bandaging simulations, offering an intuitive learning method and strengthening emergency response skills.

  • Climbing Route Selection: Offers multiple mountain paths, simulating different climbing experiences. Users can enter various information pages based on their needs.


    Mountain Climbing Hazards

  • By sliding or clicking the "Go Forward"/"Run Away" buttons, users simulate the climbing process. As the altitude changes, potential hazards at different elevations are displayed.

  • Clicking on different "monsters" (representing potential climbing risks) shows detailed information about environmental hazards in the info box at the bottom left, increasing user risk awareness and judgment ability.力。


    Shopping Page

  • The interactive design integrates learning and shopping, allowing users to purchase necessary equipment while learning about mountain climbing safety. This creates a complete experience for both safety information and equipment selection.


Through immersive interactive design, the website not only helps users learn mountain climbing safety and self-rescue methods but also uses gamification to boost engagement, making information delivery more attractive and enjoyable. Additionally, integrating the shopping feature enhances the website's practicality and value.
