/Down Go - Concept

Using pixel art as the primary visual language, this project transforms mountain climbing safety tips and emergency self-rescue methods into easy-to-understand and engaging information through gamified interaction. We aim to break away from the traditional, rigid format of instruction manuals, allowing users to absorb important safety knowledge in a relaxed setting.


Small Handbook: Integrates essential mountain climbing tips and emergency response guidelines with pixel-style illustrations and clear step-by-step instructions, lowering the comprehension threshold for users.

Splint & Bandage Outer Box: Not only does it serve a practical rescue function, but the design also includes simple, intuitive icons, allowing users to quickly understand the correct usage.

Mountain Path Marking Tape: Features bright pixelated design for enhanced visibility, combined with basic rescue signal icons, enabling climbers to use it effectively in emergency situations.


We hope this design is not just a typical mountain climbing safety guide but also an experience that combines education and entertainment, enhancing survival skills. It transforms the awareness of climbing risks into an approachable form of knowledge, ensuring both safety and enjoyment during outdoor adventures.
