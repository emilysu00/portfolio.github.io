/Guangfu VI Extension Design - Concept

Continuing the main visual style, black and gold are used as the primary color scheme to create a subtle yet luxurious feel. This allows attendees to feel a sense of prestige and importance when receiving their tickets. Through meticulous design details, we aim not only to enhance the collectible value of the tickets but also to strengthen the overall event's brand image and professionalism.
Additionally, for different lecture topics, we have designed corresponding exclusive patterns and visual elements to ensure that each ticket maintains consistent brand identification while also showcasing the thematic features. This allows participants to visually perceive the core concept of each lecture.
At the same time, the design of the identification badges extends this visual language, maintaining the black and gold color scheme and refined design vocabulary. This allows each participant to feel more immersed in the event atmosphere while experiencing the unique brand value. The design of the badges echoes the ticket design, emphasizing consistency and professionalism, further elevating the overall event's quality and unity.
