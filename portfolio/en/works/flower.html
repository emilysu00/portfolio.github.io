/Tan Hua Fu CIS&IP – Concept

This project centers on "Tanhua Mansion" in Xiongjiang, a small town in mainland China. Through brand identity and IP development, it aims to create a memorable cultural symbol that revitalizes and reenergizes the town. We crafted a legend rich in local character: Long ago, a boy lived in Xiongjiang, but one day a flood engulfed the entire town. Later, as people rebuilt Xiongjiang on the hillside, the boy reemerged as the "Water God Tanhua Lang," guardian of the land, accompanied by two mythical creatures—"Lingya," a loyal and resilient dog, and "Laifu," a bat symbolizing blessing and protection. This story forms the foundation of the brand, which was brought to life through visual design, character development, and narrative expansion—creating a culturally rich and emotionally resonant IP that enhances the identity and appeal of Xiongjiang.
