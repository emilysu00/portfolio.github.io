/Wave Typeface – Concept

This typeface is inspired by the fluidity of ocean waves. Through dynamic character variations, it captures the liveliness and surging motion of the sea, creating a unique sense of visual rhythm. Based on a rounded sans-serif style, the letterforms are adjusted to be wider and flatter, emphasizing horizontal flow and enhancing both expansiveness and stability. Stroke details—particularly the flicks and endings—incorporate splash-like elements, giving the typeface a sense of movement and rhythm that echoes the beauty of crashing waves. This design not only enhances the font’s distinctiveness and recognizability but also imbues it with expressive vitality. The goal is to translate the ocean’s freedom and fluidity into a visual language, allowing each character to embody the sea’s energy and spirit.
