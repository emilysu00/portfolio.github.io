/Usibility Test
Initial SUS Score: 66.25 (below industry average of 68)
/Pain Points Identified in Testing

We found two painpoints by the test

"1. Users expected to see a map view under the ""Route"" tab but were shown a metro line diagram instead—leading to mismatched expectations.
2. The Segmented Control at the top was not prominent enough, requiring users extra time to locate and understand its function."


/A/B Testing

To address these issues, We split the “Route” tab into two separate tabs: "Route Planner" and "Metro Map" to reduce cognitive confusion.
A/B testing was conducted with new participants performing the same tasks to evaluate the new structure.
