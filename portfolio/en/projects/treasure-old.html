/Treasure Hill Artist Village Rebranding: color (in/out) side - Concept

We aim to integrate culture and visual identity more closely into the Treasure Hill Artist Village, making it an integral part of the space's development through in-depth research, field visits, and brand revitalization.


/Design process
/Motivation
/Design Case Study

The core focus is on three groups: artists, residents, and visitors. Using the "coexistence of settlements" concept, programs like the Home, Residency, and Youth Club are introduced. Through co-construction of art and residence, Treasure Hill's natural landscape is preserved, and cultural experiences are realized.

54Visitor Feedbacks

    Strengths

  • Located in Taipei City but in a tranquil and unique geographical environment, offering an escape from the fast-paced urban life.

  • Long-term artist residency operations. The outdoor space has few restrictions, allowing flexible use of the space with regularly updated presentations.

    Weaknesses

  • Signage and flow directions are unclear, making it difficult to find entrances, artwork, or current locations.

  • Most visitors have limited understanding of the site's background and cannot resonate with the policy of "preserving environmental features and promoting art-residence coexistence."

  • Insufficient exposure of artist residencies, exhibitions, and external activities. Visitor demographics are not clearly targeted, and most visitors treat it as a quick sightseeing stop, without delving deeply into the exhibitions.

  • Lack of a systematic visual style, and insufficient integration of visual rules in the progressively developed site.
