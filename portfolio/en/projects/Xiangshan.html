/Beyond the Sea - the corner of fields and SEA - Concept

Through exhibitions, guided walks, art festivals, and markets, the project seeks to create a cultural intervention that not only engages the residents of Xiangshan, encouraging them to deepen their understanding of their hometown and land, but also fosters a stronger connection to staying rooted here. At the same time, the intervention aims to influence tourism and transportation policies externally, ultimately making this land a better place.
Three co-creation projects collaborated with different communities, serving not merely as exhibition booths but as opportunities for residents to actively participate in the creative process, fostering a deeper connection with the arts festival.

/Bo Guang Market main exhibition

In the Bo Guang Market main exhibition, echoing the overall theme “Life by the Sea,” we designed both market-style stalls and an exhibition hall. The exhibition highlights seaside life through three aspects: living, production, and ecology.

/Sunbathing Fish-Zhuyu Sun-Dried Mullet Roe:Summer Remake

At Zhuyu, we showcased mullet roe soaps co-created with elders from Xiangshan Community, recreating the scene of mullet roe drying so visitors can witness its grandeur even outside the harvest season.

/Fish Project

At Mingfa, we collaborated with elders from Haishan Community to paint fish illustrations, displayed on the fish stands used during sales. This allows visitors to experience the spectacular “fish waterfall” even during the off-fishing months of July and August.

/Haibin Proposal

At the Haibin Community Bunker Garden, we presented walking tours and activity proposals to help visitors better understand the community, while also replanting and revitalizing the small garden.
