Down Go
Mountain First Aid

Honorable Mention, Media Encoding, NTUA VCD Department Exhibition

Overview

Using pixel art as the primary visual language, this project transforms hiking guidelines and emergency self-rescue knowledge into an accessible and engaging experience through gamified interactions.By breaking away from the conventions of traditional manuals and information-heavy websites, the design allows users to absorb critical safety knowledge in a relaxed and approachable way.

Year

2023.March - 2023.June

Role

UI/UX design, Visual design, Front-end
Team project with Yi-Ching Wu and Yu-Jie Cheng

Web design

 Graphic design
Structure and Interaction

Through a linear interaction flow, users learn mountain-related knowledge within a narrative, game-like experience.The project aims to spark interest among beginner hikers while raising awareness of potential risks in mountainous environments.

Web design

Graphic design
Style & Design Artifacts

A set of portable design artifacts, including a small handbook, a splint, a bandage case, and marking tape, were created to reinforce the knowledge presented on the website. All objects were designed with portability in mind, allowing them to be easily carried during outdoor activities.
