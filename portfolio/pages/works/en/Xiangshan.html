Beyond the Sea
the corner of fields and SEA

Overview

Through exhibitions, guided walks, art festivals, and markets, the project seeks to create a cultural intervention that not only engages the residents of Xiangshan, encouraging them to deepen their understanding of their hometown and land, but also fosters a stronger connection to staying rooted here. At the same time, the intervention aims to influence tourism and transportation policies externally, ultimately making this land a better place.Three co-creation projects collaborated with different communities, serving not merely as exhibition booths but as opportunities for residents to actively participate in the creative process, fostering a deeper connection with the arts festival.

Exhibition structure and map

The project connected three local communities and collaborated with two local businesses, inviting residents and elderly participants to take part in community-based activities.The main exhibition was held at a fishing port market, using the exhibition and related merchandise to increase visibility and public engagement.
