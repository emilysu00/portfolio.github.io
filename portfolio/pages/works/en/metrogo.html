Safe Track Taipei Metro Go
App Redesign

Overview

In recent years, terms such as “metro violence,” “late-night commuting,” and “checking in safely” have appeared more frequently in news coverage and on social media, reflecting growing public concern about safety in public transportation.To create a more reassuring commuting experience, this project builds upon the existing Taipei Metro Go app by introducing features such as deviation alerts, safety reporting, and user-generated reports. The interface and overall user experience were also refined, aiming to address the real safety needs of urban commuters through digital design.

Survey and User Interview

User needs were identified through a survey (n = 160) and user interviews (n = 10).

Persona and User Journey Map

User interviews (n = 10) were conducted to develop personas. By synthesizing insights from all interview responses, a user journey map was created to identify pain points across different stages and guide the team in defining key intervention opportunities.Based on the journey mapping insights, three stages with the highest concentration of pain points and user expectations were identified.

Affinity Diagramming

We used affinity diagramming to further refine and narrow the direction of our solutions.

Design Strategy

By simplifying the interface structure and reducing operational friction, we aim to lower users’ cognitive load and improve focus and response efficiency. In response to the stress commonly experienced during commuting, we propose three new core features to support a more reassuring and controllable travel experience.

Increase Feature Visibility

Optimize the information hierarchy of the main navigation and homepage to make core features more visible and intuitive to use.

Strengthen Information Hierarchy

Reorganize the placement of information based on functional priority, reducing the need for frequent page switching and lowering user effort.

UI Redesign

Refine color usage and interface components to create a smoother user experience while aligning with universal design principles.

1. Route Planning Feature

Drawing inspiration from competitors, the feature integrates with Google Maps to improve overall usability and trip planning flexibility.

2. Safe Journey Feature

The “Safe Journey” feature provides real-time, scenario-based prompts during travel to strengthen users’ confidence in making safety-related decisions.By incorporating a safety reporting system, users are also empowered to take proactive protective actions.

3. Real-Time Status Feature

This feature allows users to actively track their commuting status, helping them build a sense of predictability and control, which in turn reduces travel-related anxiety.

Homepage Redesign
Safe Journey

  • Safe Mode is automatically activated when users deviate from their planned route or travel during late-night hours, providing an added sense of security while riding the metro.
  • Paired with a check-in system, the app notifies trusted contacts once the journey ends, offering real-time reassurance and a sense of safety through connected awareness.

    • Real-Time Status

  • By displaying real-time train location, departure, and arrival times, users can stay informed and feel more confident about changes during their commute. This feature also integrates with home screen widgets, allowing users to quickly view live updates with fewer taps and reduced navigation effort.

    • Route Planning

  • Based on competitor analysis, Google Maps integration was added to allow users to plan more detailed routes directly within the app, extending beyond metro stations and improving overall usability.
  • In the initial design, route planning is organized using a segmented control, separating “Route Planning” and “Metro Routes” for clearer navigation.

    User Testing & Iteration
    Usability Testing

    Based on pain points observed during testing, the original “Routes” tab was split into two separate entry points: “Route Planning” and “Metro Routes.” This change improved information clarity and reduced users expectation gaps. An A/B test was then conducted using the revised version, with newly recruited participants completing the same tasks to evaluate improvements in usability and task efficiency.

    Iteration Results
    SUS 83.5

    The SUS score increased from 66.25 to 83.5, exceeding the industry average benchmark (68).

    Improved Task Completion Speed

    Users completed tasks significantly faster, indicating better alignment between system structure and user mental models.

    Improved Interface Clarity

    Participants consistently reported that the interface felt clearer and that desired features were easier to find.
