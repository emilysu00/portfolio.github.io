Cipher
Book Design

Third Place, Media Encoding, NTUA VCD Department Exhibition

Overview

This book centers on the theme of cryptography. In both its editorial and visual design, we aimed to emphasize the connection between codes, warfare, and intelligence through visual language. We chose red as the accent color, paired with a minimalist palette of black, gray, and white to create a rational and composed atmosphere. The red highlights symbolize warning, crisis, and critical information.

The book jacket features “bullet holes” as the main visual element. Through die-cut openings, readers can catch a glimpse of the handwritten-style notes on the inner cover, evoking the secrecy and significance of wartime coded messages. This layering adds a sense of interactivity and enhances the theme of "decoding and discovery," offering a more immersive reading experience.

The interior design continues the concept established by the cover. All images are rendered in halftone, simulating the printing style of the wartime era and evoking the aesthetics of decoding. This reflects the data-driven and deconstructive nature of coded messages, reinforcing the consistency and richness of the visual language.

Through this design, we hope the book offers more than just information about codes, it becomes an immersive experience where readers can feel the tension of war, the importance of secrecy, and the thrill of decoding, merging content and visuals to strengthen the impact of the theme.
