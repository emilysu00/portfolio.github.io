Suncrest
Drip Bag Coffee Branding

Overview

This project explores the design of an innovative drip coffee brand, covering the process from brand positioning to competitor analysis.With a minimalist visual direction, the brand differentiates itself from competitors in the same price range through clear visual contrast identified in the competitive landscape.The brand’s visual elements are developed based on the concept of the Chinese character “日” (sun/day), translating its meaning into a distinctive and coherent visual system.
